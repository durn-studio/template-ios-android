Expanding this to international themes is exactly how you turn a good game into a global hit. Localizing your themes gives players in different regions something instantly recognizable to connect with, which massively boosts your app store retention. 

For both of these new themes, we will stick strictly to the **"Box Ratio Rule"** you and your team are using: Tier 1 is tiny, and Tier 12 is an absolute unit that takes up 60-70% of the screen.

### A Quick Dev Note on the Colosseum (Tier 12):
Because the Colosseum from a top-down view is slightly oval-shaped rather than a perfect circle, remind your developer to make sure the **physics hitbox remains a perfect circle**, even if the art has slight transparent edges. In a Suika game, if the final object isn't perfectly round, it won't roll smoothly, which can ruin the satisfying physics of the endgame!
