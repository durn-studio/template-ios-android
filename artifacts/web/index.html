What makes it different

Built for one more round.

Every mechanic is tuned for flow — pieces drop with real physics, merges cascade, and the only way out is up.

⚛️

Real-time physics

Not a grid. A custom 2D physics engine gives every piece weight, bounce, and momentum — the board feels alive before you've even dropped one.

🏆

Six themed worlds

World Cup legends. Historical icons. Cult food. Each theme is its own 12-tier merge chain with custom art and its own leaderboard.

💣

Destructive power-ups

Bomb a stuck piece. Swap your current for next. Shake the whole board. Magnetize everything together. When the merges stall, you've got options.

🌍

Global leaderboards

Every theme has its own top-100, with daily and all-time rankings. Resumable matches mean your streak survives a closed app.

Power-ups

Break the stalemate.

Four tools, every match. Earned through daily rewards, watched ads, or the starter bundle.

💥

Bomb

Tap any piece to vaporize it. Cleanest way out when the top of the board is packed.

↔

Swap

Trade the piece you're about to drop for a different one from the chain.

🌀

Shake

Rattle the container hard. Gravity + physics does the rest — usually triggers a cascade.

🧲

Magnet

One second of attraction. Every piece on the board snaps toward its match.
